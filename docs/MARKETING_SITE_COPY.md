## Frequently Asked Questions

### FAQ 1: Is this legit? How can you afford to pay gamers?

**Short answer:** Advertisers pay us, and we share that revenue with you.

**Long answer:** Our business model is similar to how Google or Facebook make money — advertisers pay to reach our audience. The difference? We believe the people creating that audience (you, the gamers) deserve a fair share. When you engage with ads, try sponsored games, or participate in brand campaigns, those advertisers pay us. We pass up to 70% of that revenue directly to you. No tricks. No hidden costs. Just a sustainable model where everyone wins.

### FAQ 2: What games are supported? Do I need to change how I play?

**Short answer:** 500+ games across all major platforms. Play normally — we track automatically.

**Long answer:** We support popular titles across PC (Steam, Epic, GOG), consoles (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch), and mobile (iOS, Android). **Short answer:** Yes, your accounts are safe. We collect minimal data and never store passwords.

- GDPR and CCPA compliant

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your settings page. We process deletion requests within 72 hours.
